# Month-end close checklist (pick what applies)

## 1. Capture (30–60 min)
- Download bank and credit-card statements/CSVs for the month.
- Collect receipts and bills not yet recorded; save them with date + vendor in the file name.
- Export sales/payout reports (Stripe, Square, Shopify, PayPal, marketplaces).

## 2. Categorize & reconcile (30–90 min)
- Categorize every transaction; clear "Uncategorized".
- Reconcile each bank and card account: book balance = statement balance; list differences.
- Match payouts to sales reports (fees and refunds recorded separately).
- Record transfers between own accounts as transfers, not income/expense.

## 3. Receivables & payables (15–30 min)
- Run AR aging; follow up invoices over 30 days.
- Review unpaid bills and due dates for next month.

## 4. People (if applicable)
- Payroll recorded with employer taxes; contractor payments logged with W-9 on file (for 1099-NEC at year end).

## 5. Taxes (if applicable)
- Sales tax collected vs. filed for jurisdictions where registered.
- Set aside income-tax savings at your chosen rate (tax-set-aside-tracker).

## 6. Review & report (15–30 min)
- Profit & loss vs. last month: explain changes over 10%.
- Cash position and 13-week forecast refreshed.
- Note one-off items (equipment purchases, loans, owner draws).
- Back up the month's files; lock the period in your accounting tool if it supports it.
